## Vision

Our vision is to build a strong and practical Front-End community where students can learn modern web development, collaborate with each other, build real-world projects, and develop the skills needed to pursue internships, . freelance opportunities, and future careers in technology

The goal is not only to teach Front-End technologies, but to create an environment where members Learn → Build → Collaborate → Showcase → Grow
